UpProfi Video Publishing Tool

About

UpProfi Video Publishing Tool is a personal automation tool for scheduled video publishing and content management on YouTube channels owned and operated by the application owner. Only channels owned by the owner authorize the application. It is not offered to the public.

Features

• Automated video uploads with title, description and tags
• Custom thumbnails for uploaded videos
• Scheduled publishing (publish date and time)
• Editing video details after upload (title, description, thumbnail, privacy, publish time)
• Posting a comment under the channel's own uploaded videos
• Reading comments on the channel's own videos
• Per-video performance summary based on YouTube Analytics reports, including revenue reports
• Multi-channel management
• Secure OAuth 2.0 authentication

Google Permissions Used

• youtube.force-ssl — uploading videos, thumbnails, scheduling, editing after upload, posting and reading comments on the channel's own videos
• yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — read-only access to YouTube Analytics reports, including revenue reports, for the channel's own videos

Analytics metrics are shown exactly as returned by the YouTube Analytics API, under their original names. The application does not calculate its own scores, ratings or revenue projections.

Data & Privacy

This application only accesses YouTube channel data for the purposes listed above. All data is processed locally on the owner's computer, authentication tokens are stored securely, and no data is ever sold or shared with third parties.

This application uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
